About the films

Each film retells one story in about a minute and a half and ends on a question to argue about in the comments: was the hero right, who was the greatest fool, who really did it?

Rivayat Cloud

Rivayat Cloud is the small private tool behind the channel. Each day it uploads the channel's own finished video to the owner's Google Drive, so the video can be published to the owner's YouTube channel.

Privacy policy

Rivayat Cloud is used only by the channel owner, with the owner's own Google account. It asks for one Google permission: access to files it creates in Google Drive (scope drive.file). It cannot see any other files.

It uploads only video files made for the channel, makes a temporary view link so the video can be published, then removes that link. It collects, stores, sells and shares no personal data, and it shows no ads.

This website sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics. The video player above is YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com); it loads from YouTube when you scroll to it, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies to it.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
